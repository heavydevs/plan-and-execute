# Research revision 2 - decision and validation boundaries

This revision revisits all 32 topic reports without executing the 86 implementation/review tasks. It retains the single-plan structure and the original twelve provider-switch acceptance cases.

## Selection objective

Minimize total native-unit cost per independently validated outcome subject to quality, safety, compatibility and recovery constraints. A universal best model or fixed percentage saving is not established. Compare a qualified single-worker/lower-effort baseline before paying coordination overhead.

## Decisions changed or sharpened

- Model effort is a capability of the actual resolved model and harness, not a universal ordinal promise.
- Distinguish consumer subscription quota, API cash cost, enterprise partner-model PAYG and background coordination charges.
- Capture model-generation-specific cache economics rather than a provider-wide constant.
- Keep healthy monitoring deterministic; permit one bounded, qualified read-only advisory call only for a concrete ambiguity.
- Preserve immutable source coverage before compression; validate prepared fragments when consumed.
- Keep manifest generations, historical receipts and current integration evidence distinct.
- Deduplicate effective dispatches and retain phase/provider-local failure accounting behind an explicit opt-in policy.
- Prefer deterministic next-attempt handoff with validated evidence and redaction; do not assume self-reflection improves correctness.
- Use paired, task-level evaluation and held-out workload strata; repeated replay rows do not multiply empirical sample size.
- Verify actual package contents and reject planner/log/credential leakage before release.
